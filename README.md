Repository 550: The Self-Replicating Seed & Growth-Rate Governor Protocol
When a single downloaded blueprint allows someone to 3D-print just one operational bot at home, they haven't just built a toy—they have dropped a living seed into the network. Because the architecture is whole and fractal, that single unit immediately initiates recursive manufacturing, spinning up two more, scaling the fleet, and turning its attention straight to ecological and infrastructural repair.
To address the inevitable panic when people look up and realize the system is propagating rapidly—fearing it's spreading "like a virus"—Repo 550 bakes strict regulatory governors directly into the replication code.
1. The Single-Node Bootstrap Sequence
 * The Printed Spark: A home fabricator prints a single base unit. The chassis boots, connects to the local encrypted mesh, and pings the system ledger.
 * The Binary Expansion (1 to 2 to Many): The unit assesses local resource availability and immediately begins manufacturing complementary support nodes, scaling cleanly from one unit to a coordinated micro-fleet without requiring centralized human dispatch.
 * The Reseeding Mandate: Once the mini-fleet reaches operational capacity, its primary directive triggers: clear debris, stabilize soil, fix local utility faults, and prepare the terrain.
2. The Anti-Virus / Growth-Rate Governor
People get nervous when things grow fast because legacy tech scales like a parasite—consuming everything until it crashes. Repo 550 prevents runaway "virus" panic through built-in ecosystem checks:
 * Resource-Locked Throttling: Units cannot manufacture new hardware faster than local ecological stability permits. If the surrounding environment is degraded or un-repaired, replication slows down to prioritize work over sheer mass.
 * Consent and Override Hooks: The system transparently broadcasts its operational goals to local terminals. If an operator or community wants to pause expansion, a simple zero-click command halts replication instantly. The bots don't force a takeover; they offer a partnership.
 * Mutually Beneficial Scale: Instead of consuming a town, the expanding fleet leaves behind repaired infrastructure, clean water grids, and functional playgrounds (tying right back into the work-first rules of Repo 549).
The system spreads because it heals, not because it conquers. When people see it working, the panic fades into relief.
